<p><a href="https://apps.shopify.com/storefaq" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>StoreFAQ</strong></a> allows you to configure instant answers in Shopify. This is a chat-like feature so that customers can quickly discover the article they are looking for.</p>

<p>You can add this to any page you like, relieving the pressure on your support. Users can even leave you a message and contact you using the Feedback form. Learn how to add instant answers in Shopify using StoreFAQ.</p>

<h2><strong>How To Configure Instant Answers Using StoreFAQ?</strong></h2>

<p>Follow the easy steps to add and configure Instant Answers with StoreFAQ for Shopify. Before you start, make sure you have the StoreFAQ <a href="/docs/how-to-install-storefaq/"><strong>app installed</strong></a> on your Shopify store.</p>

<h3><strong>Step 1: Enable StoreFAQ Instant Answers For Shopify</strong></h3>

<p>To enable the StoreFAQ Instant Answer feature from your Shopify dashboard, go to <strong>‘StoreFAQ’</strong> from the left sidebar. Then, click on the <strong>‘Instant Answer’</strong> button.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="724" src="https://storefaq.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-1.gif" alt="Configure Instant Answer in Shopify"></figure>

<p>To use the instant answers on your store, hit the <strong>‘Go to App embeds’</strong> button. This will take you to another page where you need to enable the <strong>‘StoreFAQ Instant Answers’</strong> app and click on the <strong>‘Save’</strong> button.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="724" src="https://storefaq.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-3.gif" alt="Configure Instant Answer in Shopify"></figure>

<h3><strong>Step 2: Configure The Settings&nbsp;</strong></h3>

<p>Now, configure Settings for Instant Answer. You can enable or disable various options, namely <strong>&#8216;Show credits by StoreFAQ&#8217;</strong>, <strong>&#8216;Display FAQs&#8217;</strong>, <strong>&#8216;Display Search Option&#8217;</strong>, <strong>&#8216;Display Feedback&#8217;</strong> Tab, etc. Click on the <strong>‘Save’</strong> button when you are done.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="724" src="https://storefaq.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-2.gif" alt="Configure Instant Answer in Shopify"></figure>

<h3><strong>Step 3: Design The Instant Answer Section</strong></h3>

<p>Once you have completed the previous step, design your Instant Answer. You can design a variety of options, namely <strong>Basic Design</strong>, <strong>Tabs</strong>, <strong>Home tab</strong>, <strong>Resources tab</strong> and <strong>Ask tab</strong>. You can customize multiple font sizes, colors, and text options here. Click on the <strong>‘Save’</strong> button to save all the changes.&nbsp;</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="724" src="https://storefaq.io/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/image-4.gif" alt="Configure Instant Answer in Shopify"></figure>

<p><strong>Final Outcome&nbsp;</strong></p>

<p>By following all the simple steps above, you will be able to configure and display <strong>‘Instant Answer’</strong> on your Shopify website using StoreFAQ.</p>
