<p>Multilingual FAQ support helps you show your Frequently Asked Questions in different languages, so customers can read answers in their own language. This is a <strong>PRO feature</strong> in StoreFAQ.</p>

<h2><strong>Steps to Set Up Multilingual FAQ Support in StoreFAQ</strong></h2>

<p>Follow the easy steps to add and configure multilingual support in StoreFAQ for Shopify. Before you start, make sure you have the StoreFAQ <a href="/docs/how-to-install-storefaq/"><strong>app installed</strong></a> on your Shopify store.</p>

<h3><strong>Step 1: Enable Multilingual in Shopify</strong></h3>

<p>Go to <strong>Shopify Admin</strong> → <strong>Settings → Languages. </strong>Install the <strong>‘Shopify Translate &amp; Adapt’ app. </strong>Then choose the preferred language that you want to be translated into. Click <strong>‘Next’ </strong>to add language. Then click on <strong>‘Publish’</strong>.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="781" src="/media/2025/12/image-6.png" alt="Enable Multilingual in Shopify"></figure>

<h3><strong>Step 2: Configure Multilingual FAQ in StoreFAQ</strong></h3>

<p>From your Admin dashboard, tap on StoreFAQ’s <strong>‘FAQs’</strong> in the right sidebar. You will see a multilingual toggle displaying the store&#8217;s default language. Use this toggle to change your FAQ language and display FAQs in different languages.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="749" src="/media/2025/12/image-8.png" alt="Configure Multilingual in StoreFAQ"></figure>

<h4><strong>Add New FAQ Group</strong></h4>

<p>First, you have to create an FAQ Group. If you haven’t created an FAQ Group yet, create one first. For help building an effective FAQ Group, check <a href="/docs/add-new-faq-group-on-your-shopify-store/"><strong>this document</strong></a> for detailed guidance.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="783" src="/media/2025/12/image-7.png" alt="Add New FAQ Group"></figure>

<p>You can then create your FAQs in your store’s language. For more guidance on FAQ creation, <a href="/docs/add-new-faq-on-your-shopify-store/"><strong>check out this document</strong></a>.</p>

<h4><strong>Add Multi-language FAQ Group</strong></h4>

<p>After creating your FAQ Group in your store’s default language, you can add an alternate version in another published language using StoreFAQ Multilingual Support. To do this, navigate to the FAQ you want to translate, click <strong>‘More options’</strong> and select <strong>‘Edit’</strong>.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="724" src="/media/2025/12/Add-Multi-Language-FAQ-Group.gif" alt="Add Multi-language FAQ Group"></figure>

<h4><strong>Add Multi-language FAQ</strong></h4>

<p>After creating your FAQ in your store’s default language, you can add an alternate version in another published language using StoreFAQ Multilingual Support. To do this, navigate to the FAQ you want to translate, click <strong>‘More options’</strong> and select <strong>‘Edit’</strong>.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="781" src="/media/2025/12/image-9.png" alt="Add Multi-language FAQ"></figure>

<p>From the <strong>‘Edit FAQ’</strong> section, switch the language to another published option (for example, French). Then enter your question and answer in that language and click <strong>‘Save’</strong>.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="779" src="/media/2025/12/image-10.png" alt="Edit FAQ"></figure>

<p>From your Admin dashboard, go to <strong>StoreFAQ → FAQs</strong> in the right sidebar. You’ll see a multilingual toggle showing the store’s default language. Use this toggle to switch your FAQ language and preview how your newly published FAQs appear in different languages.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="759" src="/media/2025/12/FAQs-Switch.gif" alt="FAQs Language Switch"></figure>

<h3><strong>Step 3: Set FAQ Page Title (Optional)</strong></h3>

<p>Go to <strong>StoreFAQ Settings → Basic FAQ Information</strong>. Use the multilingual toggle to select your newly published language, then enter your preferred title. Set the FAQ page title you want customers to see. This title will appear in the browser’s title bar. Finally, click <strong>‘Save’</strong> to apply the changes.</p>

<figure><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1600" height="781" src="/media/2025/12/image-11.png" alt="Set FAQ Page Title (Optional)"></figure>
