5+ Best Shopify FAQ Apps That You Can Use To Create FAQ Pages

April 21, 2026
Guide
by Jemima

Every Shopify store gets the same questions over and over. “What’s your return policy?” “How long does shipping take?” “Do you offer refunds?” If your customers can not find answers fast, they leave, and they might not come back.

That is where a good FAQ page makes all the difference. It gives shoppers the confidence to buy without waiting for a reply from your support team. But building a clean, functional FAQ page on Shopify is not as simple as it sounds. You need the right tool to make it look good, stay organized, and actually help your customers.

The good news is that there are some of the best Shopify FAQ apps out there. In this post, we have put together a list of the 5+ top Shopify FAQ apps you can use to create professional FAQ pages, fast, without any coding.

TL;DR

Here is a quick summary of the top Shopify FAQ apps covered in this post:

Shopify FAQ Page: 5 Reasons Why Your Store Needs One

Before we get into the best Shopify FAQ apps, let us talk about why an FAQ page for Shopify matters in the first place.

1. It Removes Buying Hesitation

When a customer lands on your store, they come with doubts. They want to know if your product is right for them, if returns are easy, and if you are a trustworthy seller. A Shopify FAQ page answers those doubts upfront, so customers feel confident enough to hit the buy button without second-guessing.

2. It Reduces Your Support Workload

Your inbox does not need to be flooded with the same questions every day. A Shopify FAQs page handles repetitive queries automatically, freeing up your support team to focus on issues that actually need a human touch. The less time you spend answering “What is your return policy?”, the more time you have for things that grow your business.

3. It Reduces Purchase Anxiety for New Visitors

First-time visitors do not know you yet. A well-written FAQ page signals that you are a professional, transparent, and customer-first store. It shows shoppers that you have already thought about their concerns, which goes a long way in turning a cautious browser into a paying customer.

4. It Boosts Your Store’s SEO

Google often pulls FAQ content directly into search results, giving your store extra visibility without any ad spend. A properly structured Shopify FAQs page with the right keywords can help your store rank higher and attract organic traffic. That is free, consistent visibility that keeps working even when you are not.

5. Shopify Does Not Have a Built-In FAQ Builder

Shopify does not come with a native FAQ page feature, which means you need a dedicated app to do it right. Without one, you are either stuck with a basic text page or no FAQ section at all. The right Shopify FAQ app gives you accordion layouts, search bars, SEO schema, and design flexibility, none of which Shopify offers out of the box.

5+ Top Shopify FAQ Apps for Your Store

You will need a third-party app to create a proper, organized, and good-looking Shopify FAQs Page or a dedicated FAQ section. That is exactly what we are covering here. Not all FAQ apps are built the same. Here is what to keep in mind before picking one:

  • Ease of use: You should not need coding skills to set it up.
  • Customization: The FAQ page should match your store’s look and feel.
  • Placement options: Being able to add FAQs on product pages, homepages, or anywhere on the store is a big plus.
  • SEO features: Look for apps that support Google’s FAQ schema for better search visibility.
  • Multilingual support: If you sell globally, this matters a lot.
  • Pricing: Many great apps offer free plans that are good enough for smaller stores.

With that in mind, here are the 5+ best Shopify FAQ apps worth considering.

1. StoreFAQ 

First, in our list, we have StoreFAQ. It is best for Shopify merchants who want a complete, flexible FAQ solution with AI-powered writing and live chat built in.

StoreFAQ is designed to do one thing really well, help your customers get answers and buy with confidence. It packs an impressive set of features into one clean, easy-to-use app. Whether you are setting up your first FAQ page or migrating a large FAQ library, StoreFAQ has you covered.

Key Features:

AI-Powered FAQ Writing: Writing FAQs from scratch takes time. StoreFAQ speeds this up with AI that helps you answer common customer questions faster than ever. Just provide the question and let the AI handle the rest.

Live Chat Integration: Sometimes an FAQ is not enough. StoreFAQ lets you add live chat directly to your Shopify store so you can connect with customers in real time. You can even style the chat interface to match your brand.

Drag-and-Drop FAQ Builder: Adding and reordering FAQs is completely hassle-free. Just drag and drop your accordion-style FAQ items wherever you want them. No code, no confusion.

Multilingual FAQ Support: If your store serves customers in different countries, StoreFAQ lets you display FAQs in multiple languages. This helps you deliver clear and localized support to a global audience.

Full Design Flexibility: With a visual builder and custom CSS styling, you get complete freedom over how your FAQ page looks. You don’t have to settle for a default template that clashes with your brand.

Easy Import & Export: Moving FAQs from one store to another? StoreFAQ makes this simple with a built-in import and export tool. You can migrate all your FAQ content quickly and securely.

Display FAQs Anywhere on Your Store: Whether it is a product page, homepage, or any custom page, StoreFAQ lets you place your FAQs wherever it makes the most sense for your customers.

Highlights:

  • Write with AI 
  • Multilingual FAQ Creation
  • Live Chat & Instant Answer
  • FAQ Import & Export options
  • Free plan available; PRO plan starts from $7.99/month
  • Rated 4.9/5
  • Has the ‘Built for Shopify’ badge

StoreFAQ is a strong pick if you want a complete, easy-to-manage FAQ system that grows with your store. The combination of AI writing, live chat, multilingual support, and flexible design makes it one of the most well-rounded top Shopify FAQ apps available.

2. SB: Helpdesk, Live Chat & FAQs

Next, in this list, there is SB, also known as HelpCenter. It can be a good option for stores that want more than just an FAQ page, including live chat, a helpdesk, and omnichannel support.

HelpCenter has been in the Shopify app ecosystem for nearly a decade. It is built for merchants who want a complete customer support platform, not just a simple FAQ builder. The app combines an AI-powered FAQ creator, live chat, helpdesk ticketing, and product tabs, all under one roof.

One of its standout features is that it allows to generate FAQ content with AI. You can also translate FAQs instantly using its LangShop integration, which is a huge time-saver for international stores.

HelpCenter comes with over 25 templates for FAQ pages, self-service widgets, contact forms, and product tabs. It also supports live chat and Facebook Messenger, letting your team manage all inquiries from a single inbox.

Highlights:

  • AI FAQ creation powered by ChatGPT-4
  • 25+ customizable templates
  • Live chat, helpdesk ticketing, and Facebook Messenger in one inbox
  • Free plan available; paid plans start from $5.99/month
  • Rated 4.6/5 based on 300+ reviews
  • Does not have the Built for Shopify badge

SB: Helpdesk, Live Chat & FAQ or HelpCenter is a solid choice for growing Shopify stores that need more than just FAQs. It is especially useful if you are already juggling multiple support channels.

3. HelpLab FAQs Page

Brands that want a polished, SEO-friendly FAQ page with strong product-level FAQ support can use HelpLab.

HelpLab FAQ Page is a dedicated FAQ solution built by Avocados Lab. It focuses on doing one thing well: creating beautiful, customizable FAQ pages that load fast and look great on any device.

What makes HelpLab stand out is the level of customization it offers. You can tweak fonts, colors, and styles down to the CSS level. You can create store-wide FAQs as well as product-specific ones, which is especially useful for stores with complex or varied product catalogs.

HelpLab also supports Google Rich Snippets, meaning your FAQs can appear directly in search results, giving your store extra SEO visibility without any extra effort.

Highlights:

  • Images and videos can be added to answers
  • Multilingual support available
  • Free plan available; paid plans start at $3.99/month
  • Rated 5.0/5 from 200+ reviews
  • Google SEO Rich Snippet integration
  • Does not have the Built for Shopify badge

HelpLab is a top pick for merchants who care about both aesthetics and SEO. It is easy to set up and does not require any coding knowledge.

4. POWR: FAQ Page & Help Center 

In this list of 5+ top Shopify FAQ apps, POWR is the next one. New Shopify store owners who need a simple, no-fuss FAQ app to get started quickly, it is suitable for them.

POWR’s FAQ app takes a no-code approach to building FAQ pages. It is designed to be as accessible as possible, which makes it a great starting point if you’re just getting your store off the ground.

The app lets you add links, images, and videos directly into your FAQ answers, making it easy to give customers a richer, more helpful experience. You can also enable a search bar so shoppers can find answers without scrolling through everything.

POWR also supports SEO optimization out of the box, so your FAQ content has a better chance of showing up in organic search results.

Highlights:

  • Add images, links, and videos to FAQ answers
  • SEO-optimized FAQ content
  • Free plan available; paid plans from $3.84/month
  • Rated 4.7/5 on the Shopify App Store
  • Does not have the ‘Built for Shopify’ badge

POWR is an excellent entry point for store owners who want a functional FAQ page without a steep learning curve.

5. Chatty: AI Chatbot & Live Chat

For stores that want AI to handle FAQ responses automatically, reducing the need for manual support, Chatty can be used.

It combines AI-powered chat, live chat, and a FAQ/help center builder in one place. The AI is powered by ChatGPT-4o, which means it can handle product questions, order tracking, and general inquiries 24/7, without a human needing to step in.

What sets Chatty apart is how it ties FAQs into the live chat experience. Instead of a standalone FAQ page, it serves up FAQ answers right inside the chat widget. Customers get the help they need instantly, and if the AI can’t handle it, it escalates to a human agent.

Chatty also consolidates conversations from Email, Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp in a single inbox, making it a powerful tool for teams managing high support volumes.

Highlights:

  • AI chatbot powered by ChatGPT-4o for 24/7 support
  • FAQ and help center builder with accordion and tab layouts
  • Multilingual FAQ support
  • Built-in order tracking
  • Free plan available; PRO starting from $19.99/month
  • Rated 4.9/5 from 1,700+ reviews 

Chatty is a great pick when you need to create a self-service help center instead of dedicated FAQ apps.

6. Trusted Help Center & FAQ

Next, in this list, we have Trusted Help Center & FAQ. It can be a good option for established Shopify stores that need a clean, no-fuss FAQ solution without overcomplicating things.

Trusted Help Center & FAQ keep things simple. It works independently, meaning it does not require any changes to your Shopify theme. There is no coding needed, and setup is straightforward.

You get a fully customizable FAQ page where you can adjust font size, colors, and styles to match your brand. The app also lets you add images and videos to your answers, which helps make responses more visual and easier to understand.

One useful feature is the ability to show specific FAQs on particular pages, so product-related questions only appear on the relevant product pages, while general questions live on your main FAQ page.

Highlights:

  • Add images, videos, and hyperlinks to answers
  • SEO schema fields for better search visibility
  • Free plan available; PRO starts at $4.99/month
  • Rated 5.0/5 from 84+ reviews
  • Multilingual support available on higher plans
  • Has the ‘Built for Shopify’ badge

Trusted Help Center is a reliable, lightweight option for stores that already have a handle on their support but want a cleaner FAQ experience.

Which Top Shopify FAQ App Should You Choose?

The right app depends on what your store needs right now.

If you want a complete FAQ solution that handles everything from AI-assisted writing to live chat and multilingual support, StoreFAQ can be the right solution with a Free plan available to explore. It offers a simple and intuitive experience to create FAQ pages on Shopify.

If you are on a tight budget and just starting out, POWR or HelpLab offer excellent free plans that are more than enough to get a professional FAQ page up and running.

If you want your FAQs to feel more like a conversation, Chatty is worth exploring. Its AI handles queries automatically, which means less manual work for your team.

And if you want a clean, no-hassle FAQ page that just works without touching your theme, Trusted Help Center is a reliable, lightweight choice.

Whatever you pick, having an FAQ page is always better than not having one. It saves your team time, builds customer trust, and when done right, it helps more shoppers make confident purchases. Below is a comparison at a glance:

Build Effective Shopify FAQ Pages that Truly Impact Your Growth

FAQ pages are one of the simplest and most effective ways to improve the customer experience on your Shopify store. They answer questions before they become obstacles, reduce repetitive support tickets, and give shoppers the confidence to buy.

The top Shopify FAQ apps listed in this post cover a wide range of needs, from lightweight, beginner-friendly apps to full-featured support platforms powered by AI. Each one brings something different to the table, so take a moment to think about what matters most for your store before choosing.
